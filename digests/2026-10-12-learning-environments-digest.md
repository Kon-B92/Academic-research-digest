### Generative Artificial Intelligence Use and Learning Engagement Among Medical Undergraduates: Statistical Indirect and Configurational Associations Involving Basic Psychological Need Satisfaction
- **Authors:** Not retrieved (see transparency note)
- **Source:** *Behavioral Sciences* (MDPI), 2026, 16(9), 1694
- **Publication date:** Reported as 19 September 2026 (search snippet only; not verified on the publisher page)
- **URL:** https://doi.org/10.3390/bs16091694

Per the snippet, the study found no direct path from generative-AI use to learning engagement (path coefficient 0.004, p = 0.404). It instead examines indirect and configurational links through basic psychological need satisfaction. For teaching quality, this suggests that deploying AI tools does not by itself improve engagement; instructional design that supports autonomy, competence and relatedness is the likelier lever. Read through SDT, need satisfaction is positioned as the mechanism that determines whether AI use helps or not.
